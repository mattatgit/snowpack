# Furanodake historical POC — 2026-01

## Test period

31 December 2025 through 3 January 2026.

## Purpose

Reconstruct the evolving snow distribution around Furanodake / Kamifuranodake and compare the result with independent field observations.

## Success criteria

The experiment should demonstrate:

- reproducible terrain preprocessing;
- reproducible historical weather forcing;
- a time-evolving snow-state grid;
- physically plausible response to elevation, wind, temperature and solar forcing;
- comparison with JAN observations;
- explicit confidence/provenance.

Exact snow depth at arbitrary points is **not** the primary success criterion for v0.
